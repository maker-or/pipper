# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI developers, product managers, and indie developers who use the app as a lightweight control surface for agent runs. They are usually in an active build or debugging session, watching a run, comparing provider behavior, or jumping between threads, settings, and environment state.

## Product Purpose

Provide a minimal, reliable GUI around cloud coding agents like Codex, Claude, and OpenCode. The product exists to make agent sessions easy to start, easy to monitor, and easy to recover when something goes wrong. Success means the user can keep momentum without fighting the tool.

## Brand Personality

Technical, calm, dependable.

The voice should feel direct and precise, with low ceremony and no hype. It should help users understand state quickly, trust what the app is doing, and recover from failures without confusion.

## Anti-references

- Loud SaaS dashboards with decorative analytics clutter.
- Marketing-style interfaces that overexplain the obvious.
- Flashy motion or visual effects that obscure state changes.
- Ambiguous system messages that do not say what happened or what to do next.
- Overly playful or whimsical product surfaces that make the tool feel less trustworthy.

## Design Principles

- Make current state obvious first, because users need to trust what the agent is doing now.
- Keep control surfaces compact and predictable, so experienced users can move quickly.
- Prefer explicit recovery paths over hidden behavior, especially around restarts, reconnects, and partial streams.
- Treat provider differences as part of the workflow, not as decoration.
- Optimize for sustained use, where clarity and stability matter more than novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for strong contrast, keyboard-first navigation, and predictable focus order. Support reduced motion so state changes remain understandable without animation. Avoid relying on color alone to communicate status, and keep error and recovery messaging plain-language and actionable.
